User:
A clock shows 3:15. What is the angle in degrees between the hour hand and the minute hand? Answer in English with steps.

Assistant:
To find the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand at 3:15, we need to calculate the positions of both hands in degrees from the 12 o’clock position.

---

### Step 1: Understand the movement of the hands

- A clock is a circle, so it has 360 degrees.
- The entire clock is divided into 12 hours, so each hour represents:
  $$
  \frac{360^\circ}{12} = 30^\circ
  $$
- The hour hand moves 30 degrees per hour, and also moves continuously as the minutes pass. So it moves:
  $$
  0.5^\circ \text{ per minute} \quad (\text{since } 30^\circ / 60 = 0.5^\circ)
  $$

- The minute hand moves 6 degrees per minute (since 360° / 60 = 6° per minute).

---

### Step 2: Find the position of the minute hand at 3:15

- At 15 minutes, the minute hand is at:
  $$
  15 \times 6^\circ = 90^\circ
  $$
  (this is the 3 o’clock position on the clock face)

---

### Step 3: Find the position of the hour hand at 3:15

- At exactly 3:00, the hour hand is at:
  $$
  3 \times 30^\circ = 90^\circ
  $$
- But by 3:15, the hour hand has moved further. It moves 0.5° per minute, so in 15 minutes it moves:
  $$
  15 \times 0.5^\circ = 7.5^\circ
  $$
- So at 3:15, the hour hand is at:
  $$
  90^\circ + 7.5^\circ = 97.5^\circ
  $$

---

### Step 4: Find the angle between the two hands

- Minute hand: 90°
- Hour hand: 97.5°
- The difference is:
  $$
  |97.5^\circ - 90^\circ| = 7.5^\circ
  $$

Since the angle between the hands is the smaller one (and 7.5° is less than 180°), this is the correct angle.

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

The angle between the hour hand and the minute hand at 3:15 is **7.5 degrees**.
