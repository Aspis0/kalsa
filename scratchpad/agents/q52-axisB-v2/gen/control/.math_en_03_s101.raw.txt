User:
How many distinct permutations are there of the letters in the word BOOK? Answer in English showing the counting argument.

Assistant:
The word "BOOK" has 4 letters: B, O, O, K.

There are two O's, and the other letters (B and K) are unique.

To find the number of distinct permutations, we use the formula for permutations of a multiset:

$$
\frac{n!}{k_1! \cdot k_2! \cdot \ldots}
$$

where $n$ is the total number of letters, and $k_i$ are the factorials of the counts of each repeated letter.

Here, $n = 4$, and the letter "O" is repeated twice, so:

$$
\frac{4!}{2!} = \frac{24}{2} = 12
$$

So, there are 12 distinct permutations of the letters in "BOOK".

**Counting argument:**  
We can think of arranging the 4 letters. If all letters were unique, there would be $4! = 24$ arrangements. But since the two O’s are identical, swapping them doesn’t create a new arrangement. So we divide by 2! to account for the overcounting of identical O’s. This gives $24 / 2 = 12$ distinct permutations.

Examples:  
BOO, BOKO, BOKO, OOBK, OBOK, OOKB, etc. — all distinct when O’s are indistinguishable.
