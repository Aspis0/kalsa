A tank is 3/4 full. You drain 1/3 of the current contents. What fraction of the tank's capacity remains? Answer in English with steps.

Assistant:
Let’s think through this step by step.

We are told the tank is **3/4 full**. That means the amount of water in the tank is **3/4 of its total capacity**.

Now, we **drain 1/3 of the current contents**. So we take 1/3 of the 3/4 that’s already in the tank and remove it.

So, the amount drained is:  
$$
\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}
$$

So, we remove **1/4 of the tank’s capacity**.

Now, how much remains?  
We started with **3/4**, and we removed **1/4**, so:

$$
\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

So, **1/2** of the tank’s capacity remains.

**Answer: 1/2 of the tank’s capacity remains.**
