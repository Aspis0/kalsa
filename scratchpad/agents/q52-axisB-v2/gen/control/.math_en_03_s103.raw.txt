How many distinct permutations are there of the letters in the word BOOK? Answer in English showing the counting argument.

Assistant:
The word "BOOK" has 4 letters: B, O, O, K. Notice that the letter "O" appears twice, while the other letters (B and K) are unique.

To find the number of distinct permutations, we use the formula for permutations of a multiset:

$$
\frac{n!}{k_1! \cdot k_2! \cdot \ldots \cdot k_m!}
$$

where $ n $ is the total number of letters, and $ k_i $ are the frequencies of each repeated letter.

Here, $ n = 4 $, and the letter "O" is repeated twice (so $ k_1 = 2 $), and the other letters are unique (so their factorials are 1).

So the number of distinct permutations is:

$$
\frac{4!}{2!} = \frac{24}{2} = 12
$$

We can also think of it this way: if all letters were distinct, there would be $ 4! = 24 $ permutations. But since the two "O"s are indistinguishable, we divide by 2! to account for the overcounting — each unique arrangement is counted twice in the 24, once for each ordering of the two O’s.

Thus, there are **12 distinct permutations** of the letters in "BOOK".

Answer: 12
