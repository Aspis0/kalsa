We are told the tank is **3/4 full**.  
Then, we **drain 1/3 of the current contents**.

So, first, we find how much is drained:

> 1/3 of 3/4 = (1/3) × (3/4) = 3/12 = 1/4

So, we drain 1/4 of the tank’s capacity.

Now, subtract that from the original amount:

> 3/4 – 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2

So, **1/2** of the tank’s capacity remains.
