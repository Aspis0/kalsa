We are given the equation:

$$
x + 3 = 2x - 5
$$

Our goal is to solve for $ x $.

### Step 1: Get all terms involving $ x $ on one side
Subtract $ x $ from both sides to eliminate $ x $ from the left side:

$$
x + 3 - x = 2x - 5 - x
$$

This simplifies to:

$$
3 = x - 5
$$

### Step 2: Isolate $ x $
Add 5 to both sides to isolate $ x $:

$$
3 + 5 = x - 5 + 5
$$

$$
8 = x
$$

### Final Answer:
$$
\boxed{x = 8}
$$

**Verification:**  
Plug $ x = 8 $ back into the original equation:

Left side: $ 8 + 3 = 11 $  
Right side: $ 2(8) - 5 = 16 - 5 = 11 $

Both sides are equal, so the solution is correct.
