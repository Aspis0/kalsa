We are given the equation:

x + 3 = 2x - 5

Step 1: Subtract x from both sides to get all x terms on one side.

x + 3 - x = 2x - 5 - x  
3 = x - 5

Step 2: Add 5 to both sides to isolate x.

3 + 5 = x - 5 + 5  
8 = x

So, x = 8
