Let’s solve this step by step.

We are told the tank is **3/4 full**.  
Then, we drain **1/3 of the current contents**.  
We want to find what fraction of the tank’s capacity remains after this draining.

---

### Step 1: Find how much is drained

The current amount in the tank is $ \frac{3}{4} $ of the tank’s capacity.

We drain $ \frac{1}{3} $ of that current amount.

So, the amount drained is:

$$
\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{4}
$$

So, we remove $ \frac{1}{4} $ of the tank’s capacity.

---

### Step 2: Find what remains

We started with $ \frac{3}{4} $, and removed $ \frac{1}{4} $, so the remaining amount is:

$$
\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
\boxed{\frac{1}{2}}
$$

So, **half** of the tank’s capacity remains after draining.
