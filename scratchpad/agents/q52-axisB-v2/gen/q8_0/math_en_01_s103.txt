We are told that the tank is **3/4 full** and then **1/3 of the current contents are drained**. We need to find what fraction of the tank’s capacity remains after this draining.

---

### Step 1: Determine the amount of liquid in the tank before draining

The tank is 3/4 full, so the amount of liquid is:

$$
\frac{3}{4} \text{ of the tank's capacity}
$$

Let’s call the total capacity of the tank $ C $. Then the amount of liquid is $ \frac{3}{4}C $.

---

### Step 2: Calculate how much is drained

We drain **1/3** of the current contents. So the amount drained is:

$$
\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4}C = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4}C = \frac{1}{4}C
$$

So we drain $ \frac{1}{4}C $ of the tank’s capacity.

---

### Step 3: Find what remains

Subtract the drained amount from the original amount:

$$
\frac{3}{4}C - \frac{1}{4}C = \frac{2}{4}C = \frac{1}{2}C
$$

So, **half** of the tank’s capacity remains.

---

### Final Answer:

$$
\boxed{\frac{1}{2}}
$$

The fraction of the tank’s capacity that remains is **1/2**.
