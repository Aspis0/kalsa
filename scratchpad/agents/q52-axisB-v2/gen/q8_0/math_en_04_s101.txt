To find the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand at 3:15, we need to calculate the position of each hand in degrees from the 12 o’clock position.

---

### Step 1: Understand the clock structure

- A clock is a circle, so it has 360 degrees.
- There are 12 hours on the clock, so each hour represents 360° / 12 = **30°**.
- The minute hand moves 360° in 60 minutes → 6° per minute.
- The hour hand moves 30° per hour, but also moves continuously as minutes pass → 0.5° per minute (since 30° / 60 = 0.5°).

---

### Step 2: Find the position of the minute hand at 3:15

- At 15 minutes, the minute hand is at:  
  15 × 6° = **90°** from 12 o’clock.

---

### Step 3: Find the position of the hour hand at 3:15

- At 3:00, the hour hand is at 3 × 30° = **90°**.
- But by 3:15, the hour hand has moved further:  
  15 minutes × 0.5° per minute = 7.5°  
  So, hour hand is at:  
  90° + 7.5° = **97.5°**

---

### Step 4: Find the angle between the two hands

- Minute hand: 90°  
- Hour hand: 97.5°  
- Difference: |97.5° - 90°| = **7.5°**

Since the angle between the hands is always the smaller one, and 7.5° is less than 180°, this is the correct angle.

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

The angle between the hour hand and the minute hand at 3:15 is **7.5 degrees**.
