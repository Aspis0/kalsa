To find the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand at 3:15, we need to calculate the positions of both hands in degrees from the 12 o’clock position.

---

### Step 1: Understand the clock layout

- A full circle is 360 degrees.
- The clock is divided into 12 hours, so each hour represents 360° / 12 = **30°**.
- Each minute represents 6° (since 360° / 60 minutes = 6° per minute).

---

### Step 2: Position of the minute hand at 15 minutes

- The minute hand moves 6° per minute.
- At 15 minutes, it is at:  
  **15 × 6° = 90°**  
  (from the 12 o’clock position).

---

### Step 3: Position of the hour hand at 3:15

- The hour hand moves 30° per hour, but also moves gradually as the minutes pass.
- At 3:00, the hour hand is at **3 × 30° = 90°**.
- But at 3:15, it has moved **15 minutes** toward 4:00.
- The hour hand moves 0.5° per minute (since 30° per hour ÷ 60 minutes = 0.5° per minute).
- So in 15 minutes, it moves:  
  **15 × 0.5° = 7.5°**

- Therefore, at 3:15, the hour hand is at:  
  **90° + 7.5° = 97.5°**

---

### Step 4: Find the angle between the two hands

- Minute hand: 90°
- Hour hand: 97.5°
- Difference:  
  **97.5° - 90° = 7.5°**

Since the angle between the hands is always taken as the smaller one (and 7.5° is less than 180°), this is the answer.

---

### Final Answer:

The angle between the hour hand and the minute hand at 3:15 is **7.5 degrees**.
