We are told that the tank is **3/4 full** and then **1/3 of the current contents** are drained. We need to find **what fraction of the tank’s capacity remains** after this drainage.

---

### Step 1: Understand the initial amount

The tank is **3/4 full**. That means the amount of liquid in the tank is:

$$
\frac{3}{4} \text{ of the tank's capacity}
$$

---

### Step 2: Drain 1/3 of the current contents

We are draining **1/3 of the current contents**. So the amount drained is:

$$
\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}
$$

So, **1/4 of the tank’s capacity** is drained.

---

### Step 3: Subtract the drained amount from the initial amount

Initial amount: $ \frac{3}{4} $

Drained: $ \frac{1}{4} $

Remaining amount:

$$
\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
\boxed{\frac{1}{2}}
$$

So, **half of the tank’s capacity remains** after draining 1/3 of the current contents.
