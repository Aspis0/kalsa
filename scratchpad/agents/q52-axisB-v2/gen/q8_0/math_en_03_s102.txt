The word "BOOK" has 4 letters: B, O, O, K.  
Note that the letter "O" appears twice, while the other letters (B and K) are unique.

To find the number of distinct permutations, we use the formula for permutations of a multiset:

$$
\frac{n!}{k_1! \cdot k_2! \cdot \dots \cdot k_m!}
$$

where $ n $ is the total number of letters, and $ k_i $ is the frequency of each repeated letter.

Here, $ n = 4 $, and the letter "O" is repeated twice, so:

$$
\frac{4!}{2!} = \frac{24}{2} = 12
$$

So, there are 12 distinct permutations of the letters in "BOOK".

**Counting argument:**

We can think of this as arranging 4 positions. First, choose positions for the two O's. The number of ways to choose 2 positions out of 4 for the O's is:

$$
\binom{4}{2} = 6
$$

Then, the remaining 2 positions are filled by the letters B and K, which can be arranged in $ 2! = 2 $ ways.

So total distinct permutations:

$$
\binom{4}{2} \times 2! = 6 \times 2 = 12
$$

This confirms the result.

Answer: There are 12 distinct permutations of the letters in the word BOOK.
