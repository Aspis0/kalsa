We start with the tank being 3/4 full. That means the amount of water in the tank is 3/4 of its total capacity.

Now, we drain 1/3 of the current contents. So, we remove 1/3 of the 3/4 that’s in the tank.

Let’s calculate how much is drained:

\[
\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1 \times 3}{3 \times 4} = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}
\]

So, we drain 1/4 of the tank’s capacity.

Now, subtract that from the original amount:

\[
\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}
\]

Therefore, after draining 1/3 of the current contents, **1/2** of the tank’s capacity remains.

Answer: **1/2** of the tank's capacity remains.
