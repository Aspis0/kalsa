=== CORRECTION (same day, session 792790, unit 41c5710f) ===
The root cause was OURS, not the unit's: putting /vendor/lib64 in LD_LIBRARY_PATH
bypasses the sphal linker namespace, so libOpenCL's internal dlopen of libCB.so
resolves against system libs and fails (the libbinder_ndk symbol error above).
Without /vendor/lib64 (LD_LIBRARY_PATH=kit dir only, for libomp.so) the same
binary initializes OpenCL fine on unit 41c5710f: 'selected platform: QUALCOMM
Snapdragon(TM)', Adreno 830, Compiler E031.47.18.47. Unit eb49fb9d was very
likely healthy too; session 792778 was ended on a wrong diagnosis (~30 min lost).
The A1 CPU-fallback exhibit remains valid as a silent-fallback demonstration.
