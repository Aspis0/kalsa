When you use your phone a lot on a hot day, the battery drains quickly and the screen might seem to lose charge faster than usual. To protect the battery from overheating, the phone's operating system slows down the processor automatically. This helps keep the device running smoothly while preventing the battery from overheating or dying too fast.
