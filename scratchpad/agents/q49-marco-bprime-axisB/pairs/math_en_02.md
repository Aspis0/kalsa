## Prompt

Solve for x: x + 3 = 2x - 5. Answer in English showing the algebra and the value of x.

## Response A

We are given the equation:

$$
x + 3 = 2x - 5
$$

Our goal is to solve for $ x $.

### Step 1: Get all terms involving $ x $ on one side and constants on the other.

Subtract $ x $ from both sides:

$$
x + 3 - x = 2x - 5 - x
$$

This simplifies to:

$$
3 = x - 5
$$

### Step 2: Solve for $ x $ by adding 5 to both sides:

$$
3 + 5 = x - 5 + 5
$$

$$
8 = x
$$

### Final Answer:

$$
x = 8
$$

### Verification:

Plug $ x = 8 $ back into the original equation:

Left side: $ x + 3 = 8 + 3 = 11 $

Right side: $ 2x - 5 = 2(8) - 5 = 16 - 5 = 11 $

Both sides are equal — so the solution is correct.

✅ **Answer: $ x = 8 $**
