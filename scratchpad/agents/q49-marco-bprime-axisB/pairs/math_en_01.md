## Prompt

A tank is 3/4 full. You drain 1/3 of the current contents. What fraction of the tank's capacity remains? Answer in English with steps.

## Response A

We are told that the tank is **3/4 full** and then **1/3 of the current contents** are drained. We need to find what fraction of the tank’s capacity remains after this draining.

---

### Step 1: Understand the initial amount
The tank is **3/4 full**. So, the amount of liquid in the tank is:

$$
\frac{3}{4} \text{ of the tank's capacity}
$$

---

### Step 2: Calculate how much is drained
We drain **1/3 of the current contents**. So, the amount drained is:

$$
\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}
$$

So, **1/4 of the tank’s capacity** is drained.

---

### Step 3: Subtract the drained amount from the original amount
The remaining amount is:

$$
\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

---

### Final Answer:
After draining 1/3 of the current contents, **1/2** of the tank’s capacity remains.

$$
\boxed{\frac{1}{2}}
$$
