## 1. Turning it on; device eligibility

In Settings, open **Advanced** and enable **Governor**. The Advanced page is opened from Settings home, and its Switch calls `handleToggleGovernor`; that persists `kalsa.governor.enabled`. An unset key reads as off (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/screens/SettingsScreen.tsx:1477-1482,2622-2628,527-532`; `kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/governorRuntime.ts:3,15-21`). Select **LFM2.5 2.6B** as the model (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/ModelRegistry.ts:272-300`).

The load gate requires: the toggle enabled; a valid thermal sample (`sensor_valid`); `governorBase.enabled`; `gpu_fit !== "NoFit"`; no `mmprojPath`; and no expert streaming (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/LlamaService.ts:2357-2360,2419-2447`). A rejected governor load stays CPU-only (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/LlamaService.ts:2546-2551`). The battery module considers the sensor valid when the battery is present and temperature is positive, and supplies `t_idle_valid` plus the raw battery-temperature sample (`kalsa-wt-iroh/native/GovernorBatteryModule.kt:55-70`).

LFM2.5-2.6B is hybrid, text-only (no mmproj), and has `kvBytesPerToken: 6656` at its catalog cache profile (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/ModelRegistry.ts:284-293`). Missing/zero KV price returns no lane; the fit calculation also rejects unknown generations, unknown memory, or a failed memory estimate (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/governorInputs.ts:82-85,118-136`). The model's recommendation is low/mid and its minimum tier is low (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/ModelRegistry.ts:299-300`); RAM tier recommendations are advisory, not model identity gates (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/contextProfile.ts:64-70`). There is no current `governor-off-for-high-tier` predicate in the APK source. That historical concern described the then high-tier recommended Qwen model being blocked by absent KV pricing and mmproj; the LFM entry now has KV pricing and no mmproj (`kalsa-moe-experiments/scratchpad/QDC-GPU-GOVERNOR-MAP-2026-09-16.md:208,212-216`; `kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/ModelRegistry.ts:284-293`).

- **S23 (SM8550 / V73): eligible conditionally today.** V73 is allowlisted in the app (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/governorInputs.ts:11-25,60-72,190-205`). The S23 still needs a valid sensor and enough current `MemAvailable`. The app prices the ship-model lane at about 4,358.70 MiB with repack and 2,998.06 MiB without; it tries the former first and can select the latter if that is the fitting lane (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/governorInputs.ts:86-115,166-180`). Thus it is not unconditional: below the no-repack fit requirement, or with unreadable memory/sensor, it is CPU-only.
- **Jelly Star (Helio G99): not eligible in this APK.** Its SoC does not map to V73/V75/V79, so `generationFor` yields `Unknown`; that both disables the production allowlist and makes `laneFit` return `NoFit` irrespective of RAM (`kalsa-wt-iroh/src/engine/governorInputs.ts:11-25,60-73,118-136,190-210`).
