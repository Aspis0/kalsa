# archived — frozen history

Everything under this directory is frozen. Nothing here is guidance: the numbers, paths and
conclusions were true on the date the document was written, and they are kept that way on
purpose.

The archive has grown in batches. The most recent one, on 2026-09-20, moved the dated energy
findings out of `docs/` and the hostile audits out of the repository root:

- `docs/ENERGY-FRAMEWORK-STATUS.md`, `docs/ENERGY-PERPHASE-FINDINGS.md`,
  `docs/NGRAM-SPEC-FINDINGS.md`, `docs/CORE-PLACEMENT-PILOT-2026-09-16.md` and
  `docs/S23-ENERGY-BLOCKER.md` moved to `archived/docs/`
- the root `ENERGY-*.md` audits moved to `archived/`

Documents here therefore reference **pre-move paths**. When a path in this directory does not
resolve, prefix it with `archived/`: a `docs/X.md` is now `archived/docs/X.md`, and a root
`ENERGY-*.md` is now `archived/ENERGY-*.md`. Two files stayed live and are **not** here —
`docs/ENERGY-SCHEMA.md` (the metric contract, cited by the energy scripts) and
`docs/KV-WINDOW-AND-THERMAL-2026-09-17.md`.

The bodies are not rewritten to match the new locations. A frozen audit records the tree of
its own date, path references included; a reader following one should apply the mapping above
rather than expect the text to have been updated.
