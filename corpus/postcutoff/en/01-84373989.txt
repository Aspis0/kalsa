Ambrish Verma (born 4 July 1989) is an Indian actor, writer, and director primarily known for his work in Hindi-language web series and digital media. He is widely recognized for his creative collaborations with The Viral Fever (TVF), writing, directing, and starring in acclaimed digital drama and thriller series such as Dude (2021), NCR Days (2022), and Sapne vs Everyone (2023–2026).


== Early life and education ==
Ambrish Verma was born on 4 July 1989 in Gurgaon, Haryana, into a middle-class family. He completed his foundational schooling at Blue Bells Model School, Gurgaon, and Delhi Public School, R.K. Puram, New Delhi.
Displaying an inclination toward academics before entering the creative sector, Verma pursued a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Mechanical Engineering from the Delhi College of Engineering (now Delhi Technological University - DTU). He subsequently earned a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Marketing from the Management Development Institute (MDI), Gurgaon. Before transitioning fully into acting and content creation, he worked in corporate marketing and media management roles, including a stint with the Times Group.


== Career ==


=== Early beginnings and corporate transition (2017–2020) ===
Verma began his journey in the digital ecosystem around 2017, initially participating in theatre and appearing in minor or supporting roles across various YouTube sketches and web series, including The Aam Aadmi Family and Wrong Number (2019). While working in the marketing division of The Viral Fever (TVF), he gained critical insight into digital audience behavior and content production algorithms. In 2019, he chose to step away from his corporate career to focus entirely on acting, writing, and independent digital filmmaking.


=== Breakthrough with Dude and NCR Days (2021–2022) ===
Verma achieved widespread recognition as a "triple threat" (actor, writer, and director) with the release of the detective-drama mini-series Dude in 2021, where he played the lead role of Uttam Dhamija. The project was praised for its sleek production style and neo-noir narrative structure on a digital budget.
He followed this up by writing, directing, and starring in NCR Days (2022), a coming-of-age drama rooted in the cultural nuances, academic pressures, and lifestyle of the National Capital Region. The series struck a deep chord with youth audiences and cemented his reputation as a grounded storyteller.


=== Mainstream success with Sapne vs Everyone (2023–present) ===
In 2023, Verma created, directed, and starred in the high-stakes drama series Sapne vs Everyone. Portraying the intense protagonist Jimmy Mehta, the show explored themes of corporate greed, institutional corruption, ambition, and revenge. The series received critical acclaim and high global IMDb ratings, subsequently expanding its reach to mainstream streaming platforms like Amazon Prime Video. Its second season premiered in 2026, further extending his footprint in major OTT media.
