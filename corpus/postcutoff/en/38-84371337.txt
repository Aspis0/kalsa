Shimo-Shakujii (下石神井) is a neighborhood of Nerima Ward in Tokyo, Japan. The residential address system has been implemented throughout the area since 1 August 1973. Its current administrative divisions extend from Shimo-Shakujii 1st Street to 6th Street (丁目, chōme).


== Geography ==
Located in the southwestern part of Nerima Ward, Shimo-Shakujii borders the following neighborhoods clockwise starting from the east: Minami-Tanaka (Nerima Ward), Igusa (Suginami Ward), Kami-Igusa (Suginami Ward), Kami-Shakujii-Minamichō (Nerima Ward), Kami-Shakujii (Nerima Ward), and Shakujiimachi (Nerima Ward).
The northern border of the neighborhood aligns with the Shakujii River, the southern border with Senkawa-dōri Avenue, and the western border with Igusa-dōri Avenue.

== History ==
Shimo-Shakujii originated as Shimo-Shakujii Village in Toshima District, Musashi Province. On May 1, 1889, with the introduction of the municipal system (町村制, chōsonsei), Shimo-Shakujii Village became Ōaza Shimo-Shakujii within Shakujii Village. 
On October 1, 1932, upon incorporation into Tokyo City, the area was designated as Shimo-Shakujii 1st and 2nd Streets within Itabashi Ward. At that time, Shimo-Shakujii 1st and 2nd Streets covered a vast area encompassing present-day Shimo-Shakujii 1st to 6th Street, Shakujiimachi 1 to 8th Street, and Kami-Shakujii-Minamichō.
On January 1, 1970, with the implementation of the residential addressing system, the majority of the former Shimo-Shakujii 2nd Street was reclassified as Shakujiimachi 1 to 8th Street. On August 1, 1973, another implementation reorganized most of the former Shimo-Shakujii 1st Street alongside parts of Minamitanakachō, creating the present-day Shimo-Shakujii 1st to 6th Streets. The old and new Shimo-Shakujii 1st Street designations coexisted until June 1, 1984, when the remaining portion of the former Shimo-Shakujii 1st Street was renamed Kami-Shakujii-Minamichō.


== Transportation ==


=== Railways ===
There are no railway stations located directly within the neighborhood.


=== Bus ===
Seibu Bus (Nerima Depot) operates route buses in the surrounding area.
Seibu Bus (Kami-Shakujii Depot) operates route buses in the surrounding area.
Kanto Bus (Asagaya Depot) operates route buses in the surrounding area.
Frequent bus services connect the neighborhood with Shakujii-kōen Station and Ogikubo Station. Bus routes wrap around the district, operating along Igusa-dōri Avenue via Kami-Igusa Station to the west and Waseda-dōri Avenue via Iogi Station to the east.
When traveling toward Ogikubo Station via Kami-Igusa Station, some commuters walk to the bus stop on the south side of Kami-Igusa Station to avoid railroad crossing traffic delays.
Bicycle commuting directly to Chūō Line stations such as Ogikubo Station is also common among local residents.

== Facilities ==


=== Shimo-Shakujii 4th Street ===
Chihiro Art Museum Tokyo, established in 1977 at the site of artist Chihiro Iwasaki's former home and studio.
Shimo-Shakujii Mitake Shrine (下石神井御嶽神社), established during the Bunka–Bunsei eras (1804–1829), dedicated to Mount Kiso-Ontake worship.


=== Shimo-Shakujii 5th Street ===
Shakujii Fire Station, Tokyo Fire Department
