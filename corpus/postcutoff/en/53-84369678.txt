Mercedes Roffé (born 23 June 1954) is an Argentine poet. She has published several poetry collections and was a 2001 Guggenheim Fellow in poetry.


== Biography ==
Mercedes Roffé, of Jewish origin, was born on 23 June 1954 in Buenos Aires. She originally studied music for at least a decade as a youth, but dropped it after graduating from a music school, though she was an ensemble's Medieval-Renaissance vocalist. She obtained a profesora de letras modernas degree from the University of Buenos Aires in 1978.
She has published several poetry collections, including El tapiz (1983), Cámara baja (1987), Canto errante, Memorial de agravios (both 2002), La ópera fantasma (2005), Las linternas flotantes (2009), Diario ínfimo (2016). In 2001, she was awarded a Guggenheim Fellowship in poetry. Her 2017 poetry volume El desierto y el oro, according to Megumi Andrade Kobayashi, juxtaposes the desert and emptiness against gold and fullness, respectively. In 2022, a festschrift of her work, titled Iluminado artificio. Ensayos sobre la obra de Mercedes and edited by Eugenia Straccali, was published by the National University of Villa María's EDUVIM press. She often uses musical terms in the names of her poetry, noting that she often "indicate[s] that a poem should be thought—at least vaguely—as related to or redolent of a musical form".
In 1993, she obtained a PhD from New York University;ref name="GF"/> her doctoral dissertation was titled La cuestión del género en Grisel y Mirabella de Juan de Flores. She published a monograph of the same name in 1996, centered on the titular Juan de Flores novel. She has also taught at Vassar College and The New School for Social Research. She works as editorial director for contemporary poetry press Ediciones Pen Press.
Roffé lives in New York City, having moved to Manhattan in 1995. Roffé stated on her experiences with living outside her native country: "I do not feel at all like a poet or an intellectual in exile, because I came to the United States in 1985, when my country had already embarked on a very clear democratic process; thus, I have the privilege of having had the choice to come and the choice to stay".
