The Battle of the Rubas River was a battle that began when the Kaytaks under the command of Usmi pursued Özdemiroğlu Osman Pasha. As a result, the Kaytaks were defeated and returned to their country.


== Background ==
After conquering Kala Koraysh Fortress, the Ottoman army ended the campaign and began retreating towards Derbend. However, during this time, Kaytak forces began gathering from all directions in large numbers.
The Kaytak forces consisted of 5,000 men, although contemporary writers and the Tarih-i Osman Paşa also give the number as 20,000. The Ottoman army was resisting them with a much smaller number of soldiers.


== Battle ==
When the Kaytaks gathered their forces again and began pursuing Osman Pasha, the battle resumed. Fighting continued until evening between the Kaytaks, each of whom was skilled with the bow and sword, and the Turks. When darkness fell, Osman Pasha withdrew with his soldiers into a marsh and placed cannons around every side of the army, where they waited. The Kaytaks did not dare enter the marsh, which was equipped with cannons, and instead advanced in order to cut off their route.
When morning came, the Turkish soldiers reached the banks of the Bahr-i Kulzum. However, at this river near the Caspian Sea, Kaytak soldiers had arrived before them and had established defensive positions at the entrance of the crossing and were waiting there.
When the Turkish soldiers reached the riverbank and attempted to cross the water, the Kaytaks launched an attack. This time, when Osman Pasha brought up the cannons and began firing, the Kaytaks began to disperse. Since this was the only place where the river could be crossed, the soldiers threw themselves into the water in an attempt to cross all at once, while also fighting as they made their way across. Osman Pasha ordered Asafi Pasha to go ahead of the soldiers and cross the water. When Asafi Bey began crossing the river with one or two cannons, the Kaytaks attacked once again, raining arrows down upon them. The Kaytaks were pushed back by the cannon fire, but the soldiers suffered heavy casualties before reaching the opposite bank.
Osman Pasha sent some of his forces that had crossed the river around the rear of the Kaytaks, forcing them to retreat completely. However, the Kaytak soldiers continued to pursue them all the way to Derbend. When they reached Derbend, the soldiers inside the fortress also came out and launched an attack against the Kaytaks. The Kaytaks then began retreating towards their own territory.
As a result of the battle, 30–50 Turks were wounded, while the Kaytaks had 300 wounded.
