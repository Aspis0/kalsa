The 2024 NAIA football national championship was a college football game played on December 21, 2024, at Durham County Memorial Stadium in Durham, North Carolina. The 69th annual NAIA football national championship game concluded the five-round, 20-team playoff for the 2024 NAIA football season. The second-seeded Grand View Vikings defeated the top-seeded and defending national champion Keiser Seahawks, 35–7, to win their second national championship and finish the season with a 14–0 record.
Grand View represented Grand View University of Des Moines, Iowa, and the Heart of America Athletic Conference. Keiser represented Keiser University of West Palm Beach, Florida, and the Sun Conference. Both teams entered the championship game undefeated. Keiser finished the season 12–1, with the loss ending its 23-game winning streak.


== Teams ==


=== Grand View ===

Grand View entered the championship game with a 13–0 record under sixth-year head coach Joe Woodley. The Vikings were seeking their second national championship, having previously won in 2013. They received a first-round bye before defeating Friends, Northwestern (Iowa), and Morningside to reach the championship game.


=== Keiser ===

Keiser entered the championship game with a 12–0 record, including a 6–0 record in Sun Conference play, under first-year head coach Myles Russ. The Seahawks were making their third consecutive championship-game appearance and seeking to defend their 2023 national title. After receiving a first-round bye, Keiser defeated Pikeville, Georgetown (Kentucky), and Benedictine (Kansas).


=== Series history ===
The championship game was the second meeting between Grand View and Keiser. Keiser won the first meeting, 38–21, in the 2022 NAIA semifinals in Des Moines.


== Game summary ==
Grand View built a 21–0 lead on three touchdown passes from Jackson Waring before Keiser scored shortly before halftime. The Vikings added a touchdown in each of the final two quarters while holding the Seahawks scoreless in the second half. Waring finished with four touchdown passes and 203 rushing yards. He received the offensive player of the game award, while Grand View defensive lineman Jackson Filer received the defensive award.


=== Statistics ===


== Postseason playoffs ==
The playoffs featured 20 teams. Four first-round games were played on November 23, with the remaining 12 teams receiving byes. The second round took place on November 30, followed by the quarterfinals on December 7, semifinals on December 14, and championship game on December 21.
