  topology at start: nproc=6  nproc --all=8  getconf _NPROCESSORS_ONLN=8
  cpu/online=0-7  present=0-7  Cpus_allowed_list=0-7
  bash taskset mask=0x5f (cpus 0,1,2,3,4,6) — 6 bits. That is nproc.
  Not offline CPUs. Not a Termux cpuset cgroup (cpuset=/ , allowed 0-7).
  Workaround used: termux-wake-lock + screen_off_timeout=7200000.
  Wakelock does NOT restore nproc=8. nproc is sched_getaffinity of the shell.
