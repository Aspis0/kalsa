To determine what the regex `(?<=@)[^.]+(?=\.)` extracts from a string like `'ada@example.com'`, let's break it down:

---

### **Regex Components**
1. **`(?<=@)`**  
   - A **positive lookbehind** that ensures the match is preceded by `@`.  
   - It does **not** include `@` in the extracted result.

2. **`[^.]+`**  
   - Matches **one or more characters** that are **not a dot (`.`)**.  
   - This captures the local part of the email (e.g., `ada` in `ada@example.com`).

3. **`(?=\.)`**  
   - A **positive lookahead** that ensures the match is followed by a dot (`.`).  
   - It does **not** include `.` in the extracted result.

---

### **How It Works on `'ada@example.com'`**
- The `@` symbol is at position 3.  
- The regex looks for a sequence of non-dot characters **after `@`** and **before the first dot**.  
- In `'ada@example.com'`:  
  - After `@`, the characters `e`, `x`, `a`, `m`, `p`, `l`, `e` are **not dots**.  
  - The first dot (`.`) occurs at position 11 (after `example`).  
  - Thus, `[^.]+` matches `example`.

---

### **Result**
The regex extracts **`example`** from `'ada@example.com'`.
