## Rules of this pipeline, learned the hard way

1. **One writer per tree.** Explorers and reviewers may read in parallel; two coders may not write.
2. **Writer and reviewer must differ BY THE `model` FIELD, not by profile name.** The profile called
   "Reviewer" changed under us twice in one night; `list_profiles` before every launch.
3. **When a test asserts source text, exercise the behaviour it protects in the same test.** Three times
   a string match caught a rename but not a bug.
4. **Before declaring a capture**: check `mCurrentFocus` with *every* shot, compare the hashes, and look
   at the files you are about to commit — the images of one run are not evidence about another run's
   files.
5. **Never raise a ratchet.** Cut a seam; `HostRoot.tsx` is at exactly 241.
6. The **GLM 21:00–11:00 window was revoked by the owner on 2026-09-23**; GLM is usable at any hour.
