1. **One writer per tree.** Explorers and reviewers may read in parallel; two coders may not write.
2. **Writer and reviewer must differ BY THE `model` FIELD, not by profile name.** The profile called
   "Reviewer" changed under us twice in one night; `list_profiles` before every launch.

## What comes next, and the order the dependencies impose (2026-09-24, later)

The owner looked at the frames and named four things. Two are copy, two are features, and the features
have an order between them that is not obvious:

1. **The copy pass** — Help (which today says "la chat gira interamente sul telefono … nessun cloud",
   true now and false the day a model runs on his computer), the Pro screen (a door with nothing behind
   it), and the small clarifications (Dove risponde has no subtitle, Modello shows two sigla, the empty
   states say what is missing rather than what to do). Draft and the owner's answers:

   server path does not exist — so the screen must explain and not pretend.
2. **The pairing, and it is a prerequisite, not a feature beside the others.** The connection to Kalsa
   desktop is made by **reading a QR code**, which the phone cannot do at all: no scanner, no camera
   permission, no credential storage. The desktop's half already exists (`pairing.js`, 261 lines of the
   wizard, plus `src-tauri/src/pairing.rs` where the add path is called *the phone ceremony*), and the
   hub's own words are the ones that matter: **"no QR has ever been scanned end to end"**. So the phone
   side is half a ceremony that has never met the other half, and the piece to design first is the
   **payload** the desktop shows and the phone must understand.
3. **The room** — and it is the phone's face on a design the hub has already built out, not a parallel
   invention. `docs/DESIGN-ROOMS.md` here, `HOUSEHOLD-RULES.md` §4–§6 and `WHAT-IS-MISSING.md` §10
   there, which says it plainly: **"The room is designed and not built."** The rules that must survive
   into code: one turn at a time (a shared history generated concurrently stops being reproducible),
   rotation between people rather than a queue of messages with at most one pending prompt each, ties to
   whoever waited longest, the turn order visible to everyone like a game's, and the AI **hears the whole
   room and pays at the moment it is addressed** — nothing is prefilled for chatter. The queue behind it
   now exists and refuses; the sentences it owes a waiting device do not.
