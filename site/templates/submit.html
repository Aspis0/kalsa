Two ways in. They are different on purpose: one is a promise from us, the other is a place to be found.

Open now

Listed in the catalogue

For engines with a published improvement that passes the rules. Kalsa can recommend you. The Reviewed badge is separate and optional.

  1. Check the rules. An improvement with its source, stars, licence, signs of life, upstream, install source.
  2. Add an entry to catalog/engines.toml, like the one below.
  3. Open a pull request. A reviewer checks the evidence and the install source.

Community listing

Community listing opens soon. It is for new forks and experiments that have not built evidence yet. You will list your own engine, on your own, and it will appear in the Community section. Kalsa never recommends a community engine, but it can still earn the Reviewed badge.

  1. Sign in with GitHub or GitLab, using the account that owns the repository you list. A Hugging Face login will not be enough for this.
  2. Describe the engine: the repository, its licence and what it is for.
  3. It will appear in the Community section straight away. No person will review it against the rules.

Questions before you list? Write to {{help}}.
