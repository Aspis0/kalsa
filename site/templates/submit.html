Listed in the catalogue

For engines with a published benchmark that passes the rules. Kalsa can recommend you. The Reviewed badge is separate and optional.

  1. Check the rules. Benchmark, stars, licence, signs of life, upstream, install source.
  2. Add an entry to catalog/engines.toml, like the one below.
  3. Open a pull request. A reviewer checks the benchmark and the install source.

Community listing

For new forks and experiments that have not built evidence yet. You list your own engine, on your own. It is shown in the Community section and Kalsa never recommends it. It can still earn the Reviewed badge.

  1. Sign in with GitHub or GitLab, using the account that owns the repository you list. A Hugging Face login is not enough for this.
  2. Describe the engine: the repository, its licence and what it is for.
  3. It appears in the Community section straight away. No person reviews it against the rules.

Questions before you list? Write to {{help}}.
