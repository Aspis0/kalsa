Privacy notice

What Kalsa keeps when you sign in, why, for how long, and how to have it removed. Browsing the site needs no account and stores nothing about you.

Dated 2026-11-19.

Who runs this

Kalsa is run by Marco Gualtieri, a private individual, not a company, on a non-profit basis. He is the data controller. Contact: {{main}}

What we store when you sign in

  • Your account. The provider you chose (Hugging Face, GitHub or GitLab), your account id there, your username, your avatar URL and the time you first signed in. The username and avatar are refreshed each time you sign in.
  • Your session. A record that holds only a hash of your session cookie, which user it belongs to, and when it expires: 30 days after you sign in.
  • Your sign-in attempt. A short-lived record, kept for 10 minutes, that lets one sign-in attempt be used once.
  • A page you removed. If you remove an engine or developer page, we keep the page id, your GitHub account id and the time you removed it. Nothing else about you. Undo deletes the record. The record is not linked to your account row, so it survives account deletion: it is the only evidence of who asked for the page to go.
  • Abuse counts. A count of sign-in and removal attempts per account and per network address, so one visitor cannot flood the service. The address is stored only as a keyed hash, which cannot be turned back into an address. Each count lives for an hour; expired counts are deleted the next time anyone signs in or asks for a removal.

That is all. We ask each provider only to read your public profile. We do not store your email address, a password or the provider’s access token: the token is used once to read your username and then discarded. There are no tracking cookies.

Cookies

Two cookies, both strictly necessary, both set only when you sign in. __Host-kalsa_session keeps you signed in for 30 days, and its value is never stored on our side, only its hash. __Host-kalsa_oauth lasts 10 minutes and holds a one-time code and the page you came from, so the sign-in can finish. Nothing else is set, and the site keeps nothing in your browser’s local storage, so there is no cookie banner.

Analytics and logs

  • We do not run analytics today. If that changes, this page will say so first.
  • Our own code writes no log lines. Cloudflare’s per-request invocation logs, which would hold the full request URL, are switched off. Cloudflare’s Worker logging is on, and performance traces are sampled at 1% of requests.
  • Pages show avatars of listed developers, loaded directly from github.com, so GitHub sees your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Who else handles your data

Cloudflare hosts the site, the Worker that runs sign-in and the database, and, as any host does, it handles your IP address to deliver pages. We never write the address itself: the abuse counter keeps only a keyed hash of it. The provider you sign in with receives your sign-in request and applies its own privacy notice. When you remove a page, we ask GitHub who owns the repository, and GitHub sees the request. We share your data with no one else.

Why we may do this

To perform the service you asked for when you chose to sign in, and in our legitimate interest in running it. You can stop at any time: sign out, and ask for deletion.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to our using it. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

  • Sign out ends your session: its record is deleted and the cookie removed.
  • Deletion, access, correction and objection: write to {{help}} with your provider and username. We cannot verify who owns an email address, so we may ask you to confirm the account by adding a short code to your provider profile. Deleting your account removes your account record and every session linked to it.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you ask for deletion. A session expires after 30 days and a sign-in attempt after 10 minutes. Expired records stop working at once and are deleted the next time anyone signs in.
