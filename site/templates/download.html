The macOS app finds engines for your Mac. Today it installs and runs llama.cpp and mlx-lm in a click; for every other engine it links the project's own instructions. Open source, Apache-2.0.

This build is unsigned

macOS will warn you.
Signing needs a paid Apple Developer ID, which Kalsa 0.1 does not use. When you first open it, macOS says it cannot verify the developer.

To open it anyway

Control-click the app, choose Open, and confirm. Or go to System Settings, Privacy and Security, and choose Open Anyway.

Check what you got

The release will list a checksum next to the file. Compare it before you open the app, and build from the public repository if you would rather not trust a binary.

What the app does

  • Reads your Mac: chip, memory, macOS.
  • Shows the engine catalogue with engines for your machine.
  • Installs llama.cpp or mlx-lm into its own folder and starts it on 127.0.0.1. The other engines are not installable through Kalsa yet.
  • A thin chat window, showing the real tokens per second.
  • Credits the developers: author and GitHub link.
